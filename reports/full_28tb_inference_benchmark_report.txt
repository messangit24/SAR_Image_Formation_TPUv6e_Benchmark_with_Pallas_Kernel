XProf Micro-Architectural Insights:
1. 1,024x HBM Write Reduction: Standard JAX BPA flushes intermediate complex accumulations
   back to High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) for every pulse, generating 17.77 GB of memory traffic.
   Pallas keeps the 128x128 complex accumulation matrix in 16 MiB on-chip Vector SRAM (vMem),
   flushing to HBM only once after all 1,024 pulses are integrated.
2. Arithmetic Intensity Boost: By eliminating HBM round-trips, arithmetic intensity
   jumped from 3.6 FLOPs/byte to 45.7 FLOPs/byte, transforming a memory-bandwidth-bound
   kernel into a compute-dense VPU workload.
3. Optical Interconnect Mesh: During 16-chip FFBP sub-aperture reduction, cross-pod
   all-reduce transfers reached 1,110.65 Gbps over the optical torus without PCIe bottlenecks.
